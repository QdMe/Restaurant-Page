AL-BAIK

About

Founded in 1974 in Jeddah by visionary entrepreneur Shakour Abu Ghazalah, Albaik began as a humble pressure-fried chicken restaurant. Located on Old Airport Road, it introduced the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia to the broasted chicken concept. By 1986, Albaik had registered its trademark, becoming a symbol of quality, affordability, and innovation. Over the decades, it has expanded across Saudi Arabia, UAE, Bahrain, and beyond, winning the hearts of fast-food lovers everywhere.
